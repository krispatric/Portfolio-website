Product concept

Not another chatbot. An AI operations layer.

The model combines lifecycle context with deterministic tools and permissions. AI interprets messy information and proposes the next action; PropertySuite remains the source of truth and controls execution.

01 · CONTEXT

Understand the property

Property, tenant, tenancy, documents, payments, service history and market data.

02 · REASON

Detect what matters

Lifecycle triggers, anomalies, recurring issues and opportunities.

03 · ACT

Use bounded tools

Request quotes, create jobs, draft messages and schedule services.

04 · CONTROL

Keep humans in charge

Approvals, permissions, audit history and deterministic rules.

Behind the concept

How I would build this

I would start with a narrow operational workflow — maintenance triage — then reuse the same context, tooling and approval architecture across owners, agents, investors and developers.

Context engineStructured records + document retrieval + service history + market data.
AI reasoning layerClassifies, summarises, prioritises and recommends structured decisions.
Tool layerBounded actions such as request quotes, create jobs, draft messages and escalate.
Deterministic controlsPricing, permissions and database updates remain deterministic.
EvaluationClassification accuracy, tool success, human override rate and resolution time.
